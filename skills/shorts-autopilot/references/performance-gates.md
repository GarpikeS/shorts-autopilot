# Performance Gates

Do not judge a Short only by total views. Read the distribution funnel in this
order:

1. `continued_watching`: tests whether the opening stops the swipe.
2. `average_view_duration / duration`: tests pacing and payoff after the stop.
3. likes, comments, shares, and subscribers per view: tests whether the idea
   created value rather than passive autoplay.
4. views after 24 hours: tests whether the first audience produced enough
   positive signals for another distribution wave.

Use provisional targets, then calibrate them to the channel:

- continued watching: at least 50%, target 60% or more;
- average viewed percentage on a 12-16 second Short: at least 80%, target 95%+
  or a meaningful loop;
- likes per view: at least 1%, target 2%+;
- any attributed subscriber or substantive comment is a strong positive signal
  for a small channel.

Two consecutive misses are a creative stop signal. Pause the same mechanism,
review the first frame and contact sheet, and replace future drafts built on
that mechanism. Do not respond by changing only hashtags, posting time, cast,
or scenery.

## Hidden Seriality

Consider these features part of the format signature:

- number and duration of shots;
- static image versus visible motion;
- caption position and typography;
- narration cadence;
- setup, escalation, reveal, and advice sequence;
- anthropomorphic object or recurring synthetic character treatment;
- final practical takeaway as a mandatory fourth beat.

If most of that signature repeats, the Short is not novel even when every noun,
profession, background, and color palette is new.
